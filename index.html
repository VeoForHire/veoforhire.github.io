FAQ

How soon can we get access to full match recordings?

High-definition interactive footage is uploaded post-match and delivered within 24 to 48 hours.

Do we need to assign someone to look after the camera?

Not at all! Our operator stays on-site to handle setup, monitor recording during the match, and pack down.

Do we need to supply power or internet connection?

Nope! Our equipment runs on internal batteries. For live-streaming, we manage our own mobile cellular connection.

What happens if the live stream buffers or drops signal during the game?

Live streaming relies on local 4G/5G covergae. Brief drops in quality or buffering may occur depending on venue coverage and local network congestion. If a signal drop occurs, the camera continues to record offline without interruption.
